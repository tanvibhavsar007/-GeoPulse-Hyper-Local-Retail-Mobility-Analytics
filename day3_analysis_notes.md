Day 3 – Weekday vs Weekend Analysis

Observations

- Walking recorded the highest number of GPS pings on both weekdays and weekends.
- Driving was the second most recorded movement mode.
- Cycling recorded the lowest number of GPS pings.
- The dataset contains 71,589 weekday records and 28,411 weekend records.

Conclusion

Walking is the most frequently recorded movement mode in both weekday and weekend data. Further analysis can compare the percentage distribution of each movement mode.
Movement Transition Review

- Reviewed the first 20 records from the movement transition report.
- Observed frequent changes between consecutive movement modes.
- All reviewed records had a time gap of 5 minutes.
- Identified differences between derived speed and recorded SpeedKmh values.
- Observed some records with zero distance but non-zero recorded speed.
- Flagged transitions require further investigation before classifying them as unrealistic or incorrect.

Conclusion

The initial review identified differences in movement patterns, distance and speed values. Further validation is required to understand these inconsistencies.
